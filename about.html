About Equitea

An ARC community space for open discussion of equity, diversity and inclusion in astrophysics and academia.

Equitea is a forum for members of the Astrophysics Research Centre to come together and discuss issues and topics related to equity, diversity and inclusion (EDI) in astrophysics and academia.

The original Equitea website described the project as a way to amplify marginalised voices and support a welcoming, shared environment. This refreshed page keeps that purpose as a starting point, while inviting the current ARC community to confirm and update the wording.

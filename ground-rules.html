Ground rules

Shared expectations help make discussions thoughtful, respectful and constructive.

The previous Equitea website published five discussion principles. They remain a useful starting point for review:

  1. Make space to learn and grow. Nobody is perfect; acknowledge mistakes, apologise where needed and learn from them.
  2. Prioritise voices and needs that may otherwise be overlooked. Be aware of how much space you take up, listen carefully and avoid talking over lived experience.
  3. Disagree with ideas, not people. Speak from your own perspective and challenge ideas respectfully.
  4. Avoid playing devil's advocate for its own sake. Engage sincerely rather than arguing simply to provoke.
  5. Respect the emotions people may bring. Recognise that discussions of lived experience can carry emotional weight.

The old site referenced the American Astronomical Society Code of Ethics and codes from Warwick and Michigan Equitea groups. Those references should be checked before the new page is published.
